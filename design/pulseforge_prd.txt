# Pulseforge Product Specification

## Overview
AI music creation studio for bedroom producers, DJs, and content creators. "Ableton meets a nightclub".

## Core Screens
1. **Home/Dashboard**: Central hub for starting new tracks and resuming work.
2. **Create (Prompt + Inspiration)**: Two-column composition studio for text-to-music and reference analysis.
3. **Generating (Progress State)**: Minimal, cinematic ritual shown during AI synthesis.
4. **Results (Take Picker)**: Fast listening and comparison interface for 4 generated variations.
5. **Editor (Waveform + Stems)**: Lightweight 5-lane arrangement view for refinement.
6. **Library**: Scannable grid/list of all projects with bulk actions.
7. **Export / Share (Modal)**: Finalizing tracks with mastering presets and ownership confirmation.

## Design Rules
- Dark Mode Only.
- Waveforms are the hero visual.
- Cyan = Primary UI/Playback.
- Magenta = AI/Generation.
- Monospace fonts for all technical data (BPM, Key).
